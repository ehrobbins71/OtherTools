Play pitch and volume with your hands in front of the camera. Video is processed on this device and never uploaded.

Stand back so both hands are visible. Your right hand controls pitch, your left hand controls volume.

Tips: Raise your right hand to go higher in pitch. Raise your left hand to get louder; drop it to fade out, and the sound stops when the left hand leaves the frame. Good light on your hands helps tracking. With mouse or touch, drag up and down for pitch and left to right for volume.
